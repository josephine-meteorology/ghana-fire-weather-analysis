# MODIS Fire Product Verification and Fire-Weather Analysis over Ghana (2020–2024)

## Research Overview

This project investigates the performance of MODIS satellite-based active fire products for fire monitoring in Ghana and examines the meteorological conditions associated with fire activity.

## Research Objectives

1. Evaluate the seasonal and regional performance of MODIS fire products against Ghana National Fire Service (GNFS) records.
2. Assess the influence of meteorological variables on MODIS-detected fire activity.
3. Explore the application of satellite fire products for climate-informed operational fire monitoring in Ghana.

## Data Sources

- MODIS Active Fire Product (MCD14ML)
- ERA5 meteorological reanalysis
- Ghana National Fire Service fire records

## Tools and Methods

- Python
- QGIS
- Remote sensing and geospatial analysis
- Statistical analysis and data visualisation
